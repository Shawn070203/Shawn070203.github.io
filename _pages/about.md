My research interests center on building flexible, general-purpose robot learners. I am particularly interested in how foundation models can operate at the right level of abstraction: rather than predicting every low-level action, they could identify task structure, propose subgoals, and provide constraints that guide motion planning. I hope to combine the adaptability of learned models with the precision and interpretability of structured planning to help robots handle unfamiliar manipulation tasks reliably.

I am pursuing an M.S.E. in Robotics at the [University of Pennsylvania](https://www.upenn.edu/), beginning in Fall 2026. I received my B.Eng. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from [The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen](https://www.cuhk.edu.cn/en), graduating with First Class Honors and receiving the School of Science and Engineering’s Outstanding Graduate distinction. During my junior year, I was a visiting student at the University of Oxford and conducted research at the [Oxford Robotics Institute](https://ori.ox.ac.uk/) under the supervision of Prof. Nick Hawes and Dr. Victor-Alexandru Darvariu.
