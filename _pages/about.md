I received my B.Eng. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the [School of Science and Engineering](https://sse.cuhk.edu.cn/en) at [The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen](https://www.cuhk.edu.cn/en), graduating with First Class Honors and receiving the school’s Outstanding Graduate distinction. During my junior year, I was a visiting student at the [University of Oxford](https://www.ox.ac.uk/), where I joined the [Oxford Robotics Institute](https://ori.ox.ac.uk/) under the supervision of [Prof. Nick Hawes](https://www.robots.ox.ac.uk/~nickh/) and Prof. [Victor-Alexandru Darvariu](https://victor.darvariu.me/).

My research interests lie broadly in robotics and reinforcement learning, with a current focus on robotic manipulation and planning. My long-term research goal is to develop robotic systems that can solve tasks in a human-like, flexible, and reliable manner. My previous work has focused on improving sample efficiency and information integration in reinforcement learning algorithms. I am currently interested in exploring how search and decision-making strategies can be applied to robotic planning problems.
